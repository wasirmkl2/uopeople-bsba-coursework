## Unit 6 — Self-Quiz (5/5 confirmed correct)

**1. When a company capitalizes a long-term asset, what is the primary effect on the balance sheet?**
- The asset is recorded on the balance sheet and depreciated over its useful life
- The asset is expensed immediately, reducing net income
- The asset is recorded as a long-term liability
- The asset is added to liabilities, increasing total debt

**Answer: The asset is recorded on the balance sheet and depreciated over its useful life** — CONFIRMED CORRECT

---

**2. The cost of goods sold is calculated by:**
- Beginning inventory + purchases - ending inventory
- Beginning inventory - purchases + ending inventory
- Purchases - beginning inventory - ending inventory
- Ending inventory - beginning inventory - purchases

**Answer: Beginning inventory + purchases - ending inventory** — CONFIRMED CORRECT

**3. Which of the following does not belong to the three main subsystems of an AIS?**
- General ledger system
- Financial reporting system
- Transaction processing system
- Marketing management system

**Answer: Marketing management system** — CONFIRMED CORRECT

**4. Which of the following is an advantage of using AIS?**
- It ensures complete accuracy without errors
- It makes business processes obsolete
- It eliminates the need for audits
- It speeds up the processing of financial transactions

**Answer: It speeds up the processing of financial transactions** — CONFIRMED CORRECT
*(The other options are absolute claims — a typical trap pattern.)*

**5. Which of the following is true about contingent liabilities?**
- They are always recorded at their maximum potential amount.
- They are recorded on the balance sheet if the likelihood of payment is remote.
- They must be disclosed in the financial statements if the likelihood of payment is probable.
- They are considered current liabilities only.

**Answer: They must be disclosed in the financial statements if the likelihood of payment is probable.** — CONFIRMED CORRECT
*Note: strictly, a probable and estimable loss is accrued (recorded), not just disclosed — but the course key accepts this option as the best fit.*
