Unit 6 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:
  1. How do depreciation methods help allocate capitalized costs on long-term assets such as equipment?
  2. What are the key components of long-term assets and current liabilities in financial statements?
  3. How can current liabilities, assets, and expenses be identified and classified in accounting?

Read

  1. Franklin, M. Graybeal, P. Cooper, D. Bean, L., Burt, I., Carr, S., Collins, D. T., Coram, S., Creech, K., Czyzewski, A., Gauci, M., Greenman, C., Haselkorn, M., Irujo, C., Johnson, C., Khanlarian, C., Lukshaitis, T., Luna, D., Nantz, B., … Woolley, D. (2022). Principles of accounting, volume 1: Financial accounting. Open Stax. Licensed under CC 4.0.
    • View the Online Book
      • Read Chapter 11: Long-Term Assets
        • This chapter outlines key concepts in accounting for tangible and intangible assets, including distinguishing between capitalized costs and expenses, and applying depreciation methods. It also discusses the classification of capitalized costs versus expenses and addresses special issues in accounting for long-term assets. 
      • Read Chapter 12: Current Liabilities 
        • This chapter explains key concepts related to current liabilities, including how to identify, journalize, and report them, along with the accounting treatment for contingent liabilities and short-term notes payable. It also covers payroll transactions and emphasizes the importance of managing short-term obligations for business success.
