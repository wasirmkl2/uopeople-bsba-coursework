Unit 8 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:
  1. How does the statement of owners differ from retained earnings?
  2. What methods are used for preparing cash flow statements?
  3. What are the steps to prepare cash flow statements?

    • View the Online Book
      • Read Chapter 14: Corporation Accounting
        • Section 14.4 - Compare and Contrast Owners’ Equity versus Retained Earnings
          •  This section explains the differences between owners' equity and retained earnings, highlighting their roles across different business structures. 
      • Read Chapter 15: Partnership Accounting
        • Section 15.2 - Describe How a Partnership Is Created, Including the Associated Journal Entries
          • This section discusses the key steps and considerations in forming a partnership, including drafting a partnership agreement and defining partners' roles, contributions, and profit-sharing.
        • Section 15.3 - Compute and Allocate Partners’ Share of Income and Loss
          • This section explains how partnerships allocate income and losses based on their agreement, emphasizing that partners may use various methods to reflect their contributions and roles.
        • Section 15.4 - Prepare Journal Entries to Record the Admission and Withdrawal of a Partner
          • This section covers the process of recording the admission and withdrawal of a partner in a partnership, highlighting scenarios where the capital structure changes.
        • Section 15.5 - Discuss and Record Entries for the Dissolution of a Partnership
          • This section explores the process of dissolving a partnership, including liquidating assets, settling liabilities, and distributing remaining funds to partners.
