Unit 5 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:
  1. How do businesses manage accounts receivable and estimate bad debt to maintain a consistent financial position?
  2. Why is it important for businesses to balance the amount of inventory they stock?
  3. How can financial ratios help businesses assess the efficiency of their receivables and inventory management?

  1. Franklin, M. Graybeal, P. Cooper, D. Bean, L., Burt, I., Carr, S., Collins, D. T., Coram, S., Creech, K., Czyzewski, A., Gauci, M., Greenman, C., Haselkorn, M., Irujo, C., Johnson, C., Khanlarian, C., Lukshaitis, T., Luna, D., Nantz, B., … Woolley, D. (2022). Principles of accounting, volume 1: Financial accounting. Open Stax. Licensed under CC 4.0. 
    • View the Online Book
      • Read Chapter 9: Accounting for Receivables
        • Sections 9.1 – Explain the Revenue Recognition Principle
          • This section explores the process of recognizing revenue and recording transactions for different payment methods, emphasizing how businesses manage accounts receivable.
        • Section 9.2 – Account for Uncollectible Accounts
          • This section explains the concept of bad debt, the methods for recognizing it in financial statements, and how bad debt expenses are managed.
        • Section 9.3 – Determine the Efficiency of Receivables Management Using Financial Ratios
          • This section describes the use of receivables ratios to evaluate a company's ability to collect debt and manage credit policies.
        • Section 9.4 – Discuss the Role of Accounting for Receivables in Earnings Management
          • This section explains the strategic considerations in earnings management, focusing on how companies can manage bad debt estimations and receivables accounting to improve their financial image.
        • Section 9.5 – Apply Revenue Recognition Principles to Long-Term Projects
          • This section covers how revenue recognition and matching principles apply to long-term projects like construction contracts, real estate installment sales, and service contracts. 
      • Read Chapter 10: Inventory
        • Section 10.1 – Describe and Demonstrate the Basic Inventory Valuation Methods
          • This section explores how businesses use different inventory valuation methods—such as FIFO, LIFO, and weighted average cost—to manage their inventory and make informed financial decisions.
        • Section 10.2 – Calculate the Cost of Goods Sold and Ending Inventory Using the Periodic Method
          • This section focuses on the periodic inventory system and the different cost allocation methods used to calculate ending inventory and cost of goods sold.
        • Section 10.3 – Calculate the Cost of Goods Sold and Ending Inventory Using the Perpetual Method
          • This section focuses on the perpetual method and compares different inventory cost allocation methods like Specific Identification, FIFO, LIFO, and Weighted-Average.
        • Section 10.5 – Examine the Efficiency of Inventory Management Using Financial Ratios
          • This section discusses how inventory ratio analysis helps evaluate how efficiently management is handling inventory and provides insights for making informed decisions regarding the company’s financial health.
