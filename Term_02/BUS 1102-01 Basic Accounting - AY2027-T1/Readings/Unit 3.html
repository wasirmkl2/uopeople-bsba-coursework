Unit 3 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:
  1. What is the difference between nominal and real account?
  2. How to prepare the financial statements?
  3. What is the process of recording the merchandising transactions?
  4. In what ways can analyzing an income statement help a business owner identify opportunities for cost reduction or revenue enhancement?
  5. Why is it essential for a balance sheet to balance, and what are the potential risks if it does not?

Principles of accounting, volume 1: Financial accounting. Open Stax. Licensed under CC 4.0.
    • View the Online Book
      • Read Chapter 5: Completing the Accounting Cycle
        • Section 5.1– Describe and Prepare Closing Entries for a Business
          • This section explains the final steps of the accounting cycle, focusing on posting closing entries.
        • Section 5.2 – Prepare a Post-Closing Trial Balance
          • This section explains the post-closing trial balance. It also highlights the importance of understanding the full accounting cycle for making informed business decisions.
        • Section 5.3 – Apply the Results from the Adjusted Trial Balance
          • This section explores the differences between cash-basis and accrual-basis accounting, highlighting why accrual accounting is often preferred for better financial accuracy, transparency, and compliance with GAAP. It also introduces financial ratios like the current ratio and working capital to evaluate a company's liquidity.
      • Read Chapter 6: Merchandising Transaction
        • Section 6.1 – Compare and Contrast Merchandising versus Service Activities and Transactions
          • This section compares the operations of service and merchandising companies, focusing on key differences in transactions, operating cycles, and financial statements.
        • Section 6.2 – Compare and Contrast Perpetual versus Periodic Inventory Systems
          • This section compares the perpetual and periodic inventory systems, explaining how the perpetual system updates inventory in real-time, while the periodic system updates at scheduled intervals.
        • Section 6.5 – Discuss and Record Transactions Applying the Two Commonly Used Freight-In Methods
          • This section discusses the transaction related to Freight charges. 
        • Section 6.6 – Describe and Prepare Multi-Step and Simple Income Statements
          • This section explains how to prepare both multi-step and single-step income statements for merchandising companies.
