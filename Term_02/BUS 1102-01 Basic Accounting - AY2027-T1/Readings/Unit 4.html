Unit 4 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:
  1. What are some key elements of a strong internal control system, and how do they affect accounting practices?
  2. In what ways can ineffective internal controls lead to fraud or errors in accounting, and what impact might this have on a business?
