Unit 2 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:
  1. What are the related accounting principles and concepts?
  2. What is the relationship between the accounting equation and the analysis of transactions?
  3. How do you post journal entries to T-accounts/ledger accounts?
  4. How to pass the adjustment entries?

Read

  1. Franklin, M. Graybeal, P. Cooper, D. Bean, L., Burt, I., Carr, S., Collins, D. T., Coram, S., Creech, K., Czyzewski, A., Gauci, M., Greenman, C., Haselkorn, M., Irujo, C., Johnson, C., Khanlarian, C., Lukshaitis, T., Luna, D., Nantz, B., … Woolley, D. (2022). Principles of accounting, volume 1: Financial accounting. Open Stax. Licensed under CC 4.0.
    • View the Online Book
      • Read Chapter 3 - Analyzing and Recording Transactions
        • This chapter covers the key principles and concepts of accounting that ensure accurate transaction recording. This unit focuses on the double-entry system, the foundation of proper accounting records, and teaches how to prepare journal entries, T-accounts, and trial balances.
      • Read Chapter 4: The Adjustment Process 
        • This chapter discusses how the end period transactions are recorded and adjusted in the financial statement and explains how to record and post the adjustments entries.
