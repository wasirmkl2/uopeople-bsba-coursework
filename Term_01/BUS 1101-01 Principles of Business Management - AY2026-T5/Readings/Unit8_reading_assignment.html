Unit 8 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:
  1. What are the advantages and disadvantages of the three contemporary forms of organizational structures? 
  2. What are the reasons for organizational changes? 
  3. Why do resistances occur for organizational changes? 
  4. How can resistances to organizational changes be overcome? 
  5. What are some of the innovative methods of executing changes effectively? 

Read

  1. LibreTexts. (2024). Principles of Management. Open Education Resource LibreTexts Project. Licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. https://biz.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Management/Principles_of_Management
      • Read Chapter 7: Organizational Structure and Change
        • This chapter defines organizational structure and its basic elements, while elaborating on matrix, boundaryless, and learning organizations. The chapter also discusses organizations change, the reasons for resistances to change, and strategies for planning and executing change effectively. 

    Watch

    1. Business School 101. (2022, October 10). 6 most common types of organizational structures (Pros & cons) | From a business professor [Video]. YouTube.
      • This video introduces the most common types of organizational structures, namely Hierarchical structure, Functional structure, Flat structure, Divisional structure, Matrix structure, and Network structure, and their advantages and disadvantages.
    2. Mometrix Academy. (2022, December 22). Organizational change management Theories [Video]. YouTube.
      • This video discusses organizational change management theories while examining how the process of changing key aspects of a business, such as culture, strategy, and processes can directly affect employees and how the business performs, based on the reactions to those changes.
    3. HBS Online. (2022, June 14). 5 Steps in the change management process | Business: Explained [Video]. YouTube.
      • This video focuses on change management process of guiding organizational change to fruition, from the earliest stages of conception and preparation, through implementation, and, finally, to resolution.
