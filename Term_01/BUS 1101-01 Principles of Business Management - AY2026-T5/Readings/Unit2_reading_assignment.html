Unit 2 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:
  1. Which are the early theories and approaches on management thought? 
  2. What are the limitations of the early theories on management? 
  3. What are the global and technological trends affecting contemporary business? 
  4. What are some of the contemporary management approaches and practices that can address the modern global trends in business?

Read

      • Read Chapter 3: History, Globalization, and Value-Based Leadership
        • This chapter discusses the history of management thought, and the adaptation of the principles and approaches in management to the contemporary business world. The chapter also discusses some of the notable global trends that affect both the style and the substance of management.
