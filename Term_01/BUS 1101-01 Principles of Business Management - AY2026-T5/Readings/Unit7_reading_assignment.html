Unit 7 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:
  1. What are the characteristics of a healthy organizational culture? 
  2. What are some of the artifacts and traits to create a strong organizational culture? 
  3. How will you describe the five signs of organizational culture?
