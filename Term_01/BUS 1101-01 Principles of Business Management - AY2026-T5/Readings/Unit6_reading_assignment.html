Unit 6 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:
  1. What are the different types of communication processes in an organization?
  2. What are the different communication channels within an organization?
  3. What are some of the barriers to effective communication and strategies to overcome them?
  4. What are the traits, behaviors, and leadership styles effective for different business environments?

Read

  1. LibreTexts. (2024). Principles of Management. Open Education Resource LibreTexts Project. Licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. https://biz.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Management/Principles_of_Management
      • Read Chapter 10: Leading People and Organizations
        • This chapter explains leadership and the traits and behaviors of effective leaders. The chapter focuses on the various contexts in which the different leadership styles are effective, while explaining the concepts of transformational, transactional, charismatic, servant, and authentic leadership. 
      • Read Chapter 12: Communication in Organizations
        • This chapter discusses the communication process, and the common barriers to effective communication. The chapter focuses on the different types of communication and communication channels.
