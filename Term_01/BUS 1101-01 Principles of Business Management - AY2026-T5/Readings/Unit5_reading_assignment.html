Unit 5 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:
  1. What are the qualities of a good team player?
  2. What are the different characteristics of a cohesive team?
  3. What are the common barriers to effective teamwork and how can they be overcome?
  4. What are the different theories of motivation?
  5. What is the role of feedback in enhancing performance?

Read

  1. LibreTexts. (2024). Principles of Management. Open Education Resource LibreTexts Project. Licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. https://biz.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Management/Principles_of_Management
      • Read Chapter 13: Managing Groups and Teams
        • This chapter explains the difference between groups and teams, while understanding team dynamics and development. The chapter focuses on organizing effective teams and recognizing and addressing the common barriers to team effectiveness, while building and managing cohesive teams. 
      • Read Chapter 14: Motivating Employees
        • This chapter discusses the need-based and process-based theories of motivation. The chapter focuses on developing motivation skills to enhance performance and maximize success.
