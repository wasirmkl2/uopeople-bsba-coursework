Unit 4 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:
  1. What are the significances of goals and objectives? 
  2. How can goals and objectives enhance individual and organizational performances? 
  3. What are the features of SMART goals? 
  4. How can SMART goals and objectives be measured?
