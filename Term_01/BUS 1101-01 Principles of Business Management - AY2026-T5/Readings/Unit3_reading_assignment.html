Unit 3 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:
  1. What are the roles of the vision and mission statements, and values in the POLC managerial framework?
  2. How to develop effective vision and mission statements?
  3. What are the roles of strategies in achieving the vision and mission?
  4. What is the significance of the SWOT Analysis in developing strategies?
  5. What are the features of the Strategy Diamond?

Read

      • Read Chapter 4: Developing Vision, Mission, and Values
        • This chapter discusses the roles of vision, mission, and values in the P-O-L-C managerial framework, and the significance of vision, mission, and values in organizational and individual growth and development. 
      • Read Chapter 5: Strategizing
        • This chapter discusses the emergence of strategies and their importance in the P-O-L-C managerial framework. The chapter focuses on the internal and external analysis to develop strategies, and the role of the Strategy Diamond to prepare personal strategies.
